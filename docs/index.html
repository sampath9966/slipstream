Why Slipstream

✗ Compaction wipes your working state
✗ CLAUDE.md rules get silently ignored
✗ Context runs out mid-task

With Slipstream
✓ Dollar cost per session, live

✓ Advisor tells you exactly when to wrap up
✓ Every skill works on every surface

No telemetry. Ever.

Every byte Slipstream writes stays on your machine. No analytics, no crash reporting, no update checks, no network access of any kind. The ledger is SQLite under your home directory — yours to read, export, or delete at any time.
